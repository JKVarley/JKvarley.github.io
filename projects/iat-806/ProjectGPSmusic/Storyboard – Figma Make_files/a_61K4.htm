Entry point

Discover the soundscape

A simple illustrated map introduces musical hotspots across the island. Selecting any region moves the listener into a more detailed view.

Setup a canvas and load an image as the background. Draw six images of musical notes at predetermined coordinates. Define six regions of x and y coordinates at the location of each note. Create mouse hover (mouse inside X and Y for more than one second) and click functions with conditionals that determine if the click was inside or outside each of those regions. If the click happened in those regions, change the background to one of the other six images. On a hover, draw/show an additional image adjacent the hover region. Images shown on hover get drawn for 4 seconds after the hover.

Regional view

Travel into a region

The map resolves into roads, towns, and terrain. The pointer becomes a tiny car while each musical note represents an individual audio story.
